Pumpkin Chocolate Chip Bars

Moist pumpkin bars with chocolate morsels and tangy cream cheese icing.

Ingredients:

Bars

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup oil
  • 1 2/3 cup sugar
  • 15oz pumpkin
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2tsp baking powder
  • 2tsp cinnamon
  • 1tsp salt
  • 1tsp baking soda
  • 12oz chocolate chips

Cream Cheese Icing

  • 8oz cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix Together eggs, sugar. oil and pumpkin.
  2. Add flour, baking powder, cinnamon, salt, and baking soda
  3. Mix in chocolate chips
  4. Using a spatula, spread evenly in greased 13x9 baking pan
  5. Bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes or until knife inserted into center comes out clean.
  6. In a seperate bowl, whip together the cream cheese, butter, and vanilla.
  7. Add the powdered sugar 1/2 cup at a time beating on low until you reach the desired consistency. Wait until bars are completely cool to frost.
